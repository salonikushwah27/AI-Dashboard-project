
# Momato Orders Performance Dashboard

An interactive HTML dashboard analyzing food delivery order data — covering order volume, revenue, customer experience, and delivery operations across multiple cities and restaurants.

## 🔗 Live Demo
[View Live Dashboard](https://salonikushwah27.github.io/AI-Dashboard-project/)

## 📊 Overview
This dashboard provides a real-time, filterable view of 1,000 food delivery orders, helping analyze demand patterns, revenue trends, and delivery performance across different cities, restaurants, and payment modes.

## Key Metrics
- **Total Orders:** 1,000
- **Revenue Collected:** ₹5,99,133
- **Average Order Value:** ₹599
- **Delivery Success Rate:** 74.7%
- **Average Rating:** 2.97 / 5

## Features
- **Dynamic Filters:** City, Restaurant, Category, Payment Mode, Order Status, and Month
- **Monthly Revenue & Order Trend:** Combined bar and line chart tracking demand across the year
- **Revenue by City:** Breakdown across Bangalore, Indore, Delhi, Chennai, Hyderabad, and Pune
- **Top Restaurants by Revenue:** Including Haldiram's, Bikanervala, Local Kitchen, McDonald's, Burger King, and Pizza Hut
- **Category Mix:** Donut chart showing order share across Chinese, Momos, Idli, Dosa, Pizza, Burger, and more
- **Payment Mode Mix:** Card, Wallet, UPI, and Cash distribution
- **Order Status Analysis:** Delivered vs Cancelled orders with revenue lost tracking
- **Delivery Time vs Rating:** Scatter plot exploring correlation between delivery speed and customer satisfaction
- **Click-to-Filter Interactivity:** Click any chart element to filter the entire dashboard live

## Key Insights
- Delivery success rate stands at 74.7%, with 253 cancelled orders resulting in ₹1,57,830 in lost revenue
- Category mix is fairly balanced, with Chinese (11.8%) and Momos (11.7%) leading demand
- Card and Wallet together account for over 52% of all transactions
